PhantomBladeMap 0.2.1

Map modes and settings
- Default: original game map, explored areas only. Native artwork, fog, pan,
  zoom and floor controls stay with the game. The survey view uses measured data.
- The local mod manager offers independent map style, explored-only, overlay,
  minimap and remaining-list settings. Settings persist in Data/settings.json.
- M opens the game map. F8 switches original/survey. F11 hides/shows the overlay.
  F7 toggles the minimap independently. F9 adds a mark; F10 removes a nearby
  same-height mark. In original mode, F4 toggles the remaining list.
- Controller while the native map is open: LS+RS switches style; LB+RB hides
  or shows the overlay. Original mode: RS toggles the list, LS+RB marks the
  native cursor. RB / ] selects the next remaining target; [ selects the previous.
- Survey controls: right stick / arrows pan; LT/RT or Num +/- zoom; LS / Home
  recentres; LB / End / F4 fits the region. D-pad up/down or PgUp/PgDn changes
  height. D-pad left/right or F2 selects a filter; RS / F3 toggles it.
- Input polling is limited to the paused map. B / Circle is not polled.

Coordinates and layers
- Original-map pins use the game's mesh-UV projection and the current image
  geometry. Cursor marks persist in image UV coordinates even where no world
  inverse is available. A bounded inverse adds an optional survey/minimap position.
- Player world and browsed world are separate. Browsing a different region
  loads that region's catalog and terrain. A remote world has no player arrow
  or misleading distance. Remote layers use observed, save-specific data;
  unconfirmed layers stay labelled in the list rather than borrowing the
  current world's layer.
- Survey layers use authored areas where available and local surveyed height
  bands otherwise. Manual layer selection is independent of player movement.
- Grounded continuous crossings record directed layer entrances in
  Data/passages.tsv. Pausing, travel, loading, large movement discontinuities
  and airborne motion break the observation. Selecting a cross-layer target
  shows the next recorded entrance and direct distance. These are entrance
  hints, not a continuous walking route or a pre-filled database of all stairs.

Exploration and collectibles
- User-placed original-map marks live in Data/native-marks.tsv, scoped to the
  save, region and native layer; they move with native pan and zoom. A manual
  image mark does not reveal terrain or automatic collectible positions.
- Data/discovery.tsv belongs to the native save-summary GUID and cycle.
  Grounded player visits create the evidence. Legacy terrain and other saves
  do not reveal regions. Pins, counts, target cycling, fit bounds and entire
  survey raster cells use the same visibility filter.
- The original map retains the game's native fog. Previous game fog has not
  been imported into the plugin's exploration record. Revisited locations are
  recorded automatically. Disable explored-only to see all known placements.
- The native DT_MapItemData supplies seven collection groups, keys and listed
  supplies, including placements not currently streamed in. Repeated container
  rows are deduplicated by save ID. Numbered pins match the remaining list.
- Completion uses the game's bell, unique-equipment, quest-node, actor-save
  and combat-reward state. Missing state in another world's managers remains
  unconfirmed. An empty known list does not claim the whole region is complete.
- Ordinary pickups, chests and hanging rewards are discovered in loaded areas;
  stable placements persist in Data/collectibles.tsv and completion is re-read
  after loading/travel. Disappearance alone is not evidence of collection.
- In survey mode, target selection centres its position and height. Original
  mode leaves native pan/zoom under the player's control. Map closure keeps
  same-region tracking available on the minimap.

Terrain and lifecycle
- All five existing PBM files remain unchanged. An additive Fujizhilin pack
  includes 145715 captured navigation polygons from 13013 tiles / 140 survey
  batches. The capture has 97 inactive or missing cells and is not certified
  as a complete world. Other packaged regions remain partial surveys.
- Additive .terrain.pbm packs contain observed faces only; they do not create
  inferred doors, stairs, room names or loot placements. The offline exporter
  is tools/bake-survey-terrain.py. Spatial indexes are built after pack merging.
- Automatic local navigation sampling continues while playing on the ground,
  including with the minimap hidden. Data/exploration.tsv is independent of
  save-specific discovery. Terrain queries are bounded; unknown terrain stays
  visibly unmeasured.
- Unreal access stays on the game thread. A single geometry worker receives
  values only. Travel and owner teardown invalidate the generation and detach
  the UMG root. Cancelled or older-world geometry cannot be published.
- Recoverable region errors retry. Marker, exploration and passage writes are
  coalesced on background workers. Shutdown drains them asynchronously.
- Updates preserve config, markers, collectibles, survey/exploration/discovery,
  passages, native marks and settings. Game saves are not modified by these map functions.
