atlas-v11/ximeng.space.json records source hashes, cover counts and inference
parameters. atlas-v11/atlas-report.json is included as FloorAtlasCoverage.json in
dist/runtime. Surface lookup schema 2 adds a stable space code to each original
polygon. Packaging converts these source modules to PBS1 binary chunks with
independent zlib streams; all float32/int32 bits are preserved. The packaged
indices declare encoding pbs-zlib-1. The shared runtime reads, inflates and checks
chunks on its worker; the map accepts at most one completed chunk per update.

share numeric surfaces; the last consumer cancels queued work, while up to four
idle worlds retain decoded data. Tile lists decode on first use of each LOD.

加载与连续追踪优化 / Loading and continuous tracking

地图与小地图共用纹理和楼层分块，切区复用控件，静态点位复用后单独读取当前存档状态。
底图不再等待收集品目录扫描完成；瓦片按当前视口网格索引查询，控件只更新变化的布局。
连续追踪在移动超过 5 米或高度变化超过 2 米后重查失败候选；整轮结束后间隔 10 秒重试。
扫描期间每 5 秒留一个重试机会给已过期的附近失败候选，避免等待数百项全部检查结束。
导航起点缺失、查询异常、暂未找到可达物品分别处理，失败诊断最多每 15 秒记录一次。

The terrain renders before collectible discovery completes. Viewport grid queries,
lazy LOD decoding, retained widgets and shared static catalogs avoid repeated work.
Automatic tracking resets rejected candidates after moving 5 m horizontally or
2 m vertically, and retries an exhausted pass after 10 seconds. Missing origin
navigation and failed queries no longer appear as indefinite candidate loading.
One expired nearby failure may retry every five seconds while the forward scan
continues, so newly streamed navigation does not wait behind the full catalog.

原图标记只为视口及 96 px 缓冲区内的点创建控件，离开后进入复用池；静态坐标投影独立缓存。
目录发现扫描每帧最多 32 项、约 2 ms；单次原生调用无法中途打断，日志记录实际最大批次耗时。
连续追踪与路线指引共用按世界隔离的查询缓存；打开、关闭地图保留扫描进度，切换世界或存档清空。
导航终点投影失败时，允许寻找同高度 10 米内的导航面；其后最多 15 米的局部路径须逐段通过
地面、坡度、角色胶囊和已知门锁检查，分帧处理。扩大投影范围本身不视为可达证明。
原图控件数量记录为 native-pin-window；路径完成及失败原因记录为 navigation-result。

Offscreen native-map points keep only lightweight metadata; icon widgets and
strokes are created on demand and pooled. Icon construction/restyling is capped

Reopening reuses the event-maintained catalog and pending work for the same world,

Collectible state uses native inventory, reward-grant, dust-gain and lock notifications,
coalesced once after 120 ms. It checks matching unique rewards, sources within

and source interaction visibility notifications. It queues cached points with
flow, node, condition or quest-tag dependencies, plus local sources.
Inventory-classified containers still check their explicit source completion.
Quest removal preserves pending IDs; closing the map flushes coalesced event
metadata and hands the catalog's unfinished queue to the HUD.
Initial entry, save loading and re-enabling synchronize once. Event work is
deduplicated and limited to 16 records / about 2 ms per slice. Without an event,
no collectible-state query runs. Movement, viewport, navigation and quest guidance
retain their own updates. Diagnostics distinguish terrain, catalog and icon readiness.

导航面也没有完整覆盖下方木桥；桥由 20 个 SplineMeshComponent 组成，
必须按曲线位置、切线、滚转、缩放和父级变换还原，不能按普通刚性网格绘制。

scene-catalog.py 从已解码 FastGeo、LandscapeNanite 和 SplineMesh 生成场景目录，保留原生
导航构建体的凸包平面。续绘只取构建范围外、接续已有地面的真实模型采样；
不填补范围内的墙体和空隙。远处场景逐渐淡化，曲线桥面保留连续显示。
导航边缘缺少真实表面支持时，在构建边界内 4 米渐隐；不凭导航断头补造道路。
续绘与导航面合成后再生成轮廓，避免把构建框边缘画成墙；不增加可行走面，
不改变楼层标识、位置判定、连通图和寻路。西盟原有建筑截面绘制保持独立。

terrain-atlas 读取游戏 Landscape 高度图、代理变换及可见性洞口，生成独立
地形底衬（L-1）。底衬不参与楼层、连通性或路线判定；原始导航多边形保持不变。
每个视口最多增加 24 块底衬，包含粗略保底层；预算不足时整层降低分辨率，
不截断可见瓦片。TerrainAtlasCoverage.json 记录各世界的提取数量。

Rectangular black gaps in the planar view came from AI navigation coverage,
not exploration fog. The reported cuts match authored NavMeshBoundsVolume edges
within one Recast voxel. The lower wooden bridge also needs its 20 spline mesh
components reconstructed with their native Hermite curves and attachment poses.
scene-catalog.py includes FastGeo, LandscapeNanite and deformed spline meshes. Scene continuation
uses actual surfaces outside authored navigation bounds, connected to existing
floor samples. Distant scenery fades; structural spline surfaces stay continuous.
Unsupported navigation ink fades within 4 m of its construction boundary instead
of inventing a road. Contours follow composited coverage; AI bounds are not walls.
Exact floor lookup, navigation polygons, links and routes remain unchanged.
The existing Ximeng section renderer is independent of this continuation mode.

terrain-atlas decodes actual Landscape heightmaps, proxy
transforms and visibility holes into a separate visual backdrop (L-1). It does
not contribute floors, connectivity or routes. Each viewport uses at most 24
background tiles, including a coarse fallback, and coarsens complete backdrops
instead of dropping visible tiles. TerrainAtlasCoverage.json records coverage.
