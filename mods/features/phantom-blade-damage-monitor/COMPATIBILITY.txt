Read-only attribute/effect inspection and Pretake/Taken subscriptions. No combat
values, equipment, targeting, save data or original scripts are modified. The
overlay is hit-test invisible. HUD teardown and pawn changes release subscriptions.
Alt+D cycles folded / compact / details; compact is the default. Total font size is unchanged.

舞扇来源 / Movement fan attribution:
GA_Accessory_AnimMovement_Lv1/Lv2 的实例字段 Total Distance、Distance Damage
Multiplier、Max Amplification 在 GameplayEvent.Accessory.AnimMovement.ApplyToBullet
事件时被读取。与游戏蓝图相同，贡献为 min(distance × coefficient, cap)，
写入攻击数据的 AddMultiplier，然后 ApplyModifiersToOrigin；不属于属性增伤加算。
只读监听并关联原攻击来源，不修改子弹或重复加伤。关联缺失时只标“当前来源”。
Read the live instance fields at the passive's ApplyToBullet event. Its contribution
is min(distance × coefficient, cap), applied by the game to attack data before the
attribute factors. The monitor only attributes the contribution; it neither modifies
the bullet nor adds damage again. Unmatched hits can show live state, not hit attribution.

七类属性分别显示；全局族与子类型族独立相乘。攻击面板与攻击修正另列。
合计是原生生命伤害结算，不等同于扣除非致死锁血、过量伤害后的生命差值。
受击修正为最终值 / P0 原始值。P0/P1/P2 展示每一优先级入口的原始结算值，
避免依赖事件监听器执行顺序推算本优先级尚未完成的效果。
特殊抗性下限启用、负抗性、零分母、缺失事件或混合伤害不能完整拆解时，
对应项显示未知，原生合计仍可显示。当前效果来源最多扫描每方 128 个活动 GE。
Seven attribute families are separate; global/subtype factors multiply. Native
HP damage totals precede non-lethal/overkill HP clamping. The hit modifier is the
final/P0 ratio; P0/P1/P2 show values at priority entry, not mid-event predictions.
Special resistance floors, negative resistance, zero denominators, missing events
or mixed damage leave unavailable factors blank. Effect inspection is capped at
128 active handles per actor; displayed totals use native attributes/settlement.
