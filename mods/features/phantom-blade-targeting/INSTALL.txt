索敌改善 0.1.0 / Improved Targeting 0.1.0

适用：Steam Build 26367565；PhantomBladeRuntime 1.3.0。
在管理器中安装并启用“索敌改善”，重启游戏生效。
右摇杆明显拨动才切目标（0.85 开启、0.25 回中阈值）。
首拨立即切换；持续拨杆 0.9 秒后重复，后续间隔 0.6 秒。
鼠标移动不切目标，原有滚轮切换保留。
普通锁定优先原版判定可锁定的存活敌人；没有合格敌人时保留环境目标。
首次锁定优先镜头中央，手动左右换锁保留原版方向规则。
目标重新可见后清零遮挡计时；连续遮挡约 2.2 秒后解除。
死亡换锁沿用原版行为和游戏设置；不修改设置或存档。
防御、闪避、处决、瞄准等特殊索敌沿用原版。
停用或卸载后重启，恢复原版；与改动相同索敌组件的 MOD 不保证兼容。
已由用户确认游戏内可用，纳入管理器发布包。

Requires Steam build 26367565 and PhantomBladeRuntime 1.3.0.
Install and enable Improved Targeting in the manager, then restart the game.
Deliberate right-stick switching: 0.85 activation / 0.25 release.
The first flick switches immediately; holding repeats after 0.9 s, then every 0.6 s.
Mouse movement no longer switches targets; native wheel switching remains.
Ordinary lock-on prefers eligible living enemies; environmental targets remain
available when the game's candidate list contains no eligible enemy.
Initial acquisition favors the camera center. Directional switching keeps native ordering.
Visibility recovery resets the obstruction timer; continuous obstruction releases
the target after approximately 2.2 seconds. Native death retargeting and settings remain.
Defense, dodge, execution, aiming and other special targeting retain native behavior.
No save edits. Disable/uninstall and restart to restore the original behavior.
Other mods editing the same targeting components may conflict.
Confirmed working in-game by the user and included in the manager release.
