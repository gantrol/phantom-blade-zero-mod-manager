索敌改善 0.2.0 / Improved Targeting 0.2.0

适用：Steam Build 26367565；PhantomBladeRuntime 1.6.0。
在管理器中安装并启用“索敌改善”，重启游戏生效。
普通锁定与弓箭瞄准均支持手动换锁，沿用游戏中的按键绑定。
右摇杆明显拨动切一次（0.85 开启、0.25 回中阈值），长拨不连续跳目标。
鼠标移动不切目标，原有滚轮前后切换保留。
敌人与环境物体均参与选择，不按敌我类别过滤原版合格候选。
首次锁定优先准星直接指向的物体，否则选择准星附近 15 度内的目标。
轮换顺序在锁定期间保持稳定，新出现的合格目标追加；手柄保留原版方向规则。
选定目标后不因其他敌人接近而主动替换；环境物体销毁时解除锁定，不自动续锁。
目标重新可见后清零遮挡计时；连续遮挡约 2.2 秒后解除。
死亡换锁沿用原版行为和游戏设置；不修改设置或存档。
防御、闪避、处决、枪械及其他特殊索敌沿用原版。
停用或卸载后重启，恢复原版；与改动相同索敌组件的 MOD 不保证兼容。
本次手动换锁改动尚未进行游戏内验证。

Requires Steam build 26367565 and PhantomBladeRuntime 1.3.0.
Install and enable Improved Targeting in the manager, then restart the game.
Ordinary lock-on and bow aiming both use the game's mapped manual switch actions.
One right-stick flick switches once: 0.85 activation / 0.25 release, with no held repeats.
Mouse movement no longer switches targets; native wheel switching remains.
Enemies and environmental objects both remain in the game's eligible candidate list.
Initial acquisition prefers the object under the crosshair, then a nearby target within 15 degrees.
Cycle order remains stable during a lock; newly eligible targets join at the end.
Stick switching keeps native directional selection. Nearby enemies do not replace the selected target.
Destroyed environmental targets release the lock without starting an automatic retarget search.
Visibility recovery resets the obstruction timer; continuous obstruction releases
the target after approximately 2.2 seconds. Native death retargeting and settings remain.
Defense, dodge, execution, gun aiming and other special targeting retain native behavior.
No save edits. Disable/uninstall and restart to restore the original behavior.
Other mods editing the same targeting components may conflict.
The manual-switching changes have not yet been verified in-game.
